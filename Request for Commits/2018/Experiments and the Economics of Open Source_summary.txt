• Daniel Bachhuber started contributing to WP-CLI while working at Automattic, where he was looking for a way to standardize and simplify WordPress management scripts.
• He contributed code from the WordPress.com codebase back to the WP-CLI project, eventually becoming the maintainer after the previous maintainer stepped down.
• The handoff process was informal, with no official transition or documentation provided by the previous maintainer.
• Daniel's emotional attachment to the project and his positive experience contributing to it motivated him to take on the responsibilities of maintaining WP-CLI despite other turmoil in his life at the time.
• He attributes his ability to maintain a positive attitude towards open source work to setting boundaries, prioritizing tasks, and enjoying the process of contribution.
• The WordPress project's use of SVN and Trac is seen as more complicated and less developer-friendly compared to GitHub-based projects like WP-CLI.
• This difference in contributor experience may be contributing to the disparity in contributor numbers between WP-CLI and WordPress.
