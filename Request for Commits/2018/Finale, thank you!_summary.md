• The show Request for Commits (RFC) is ending with its 20th episode
• The decision to end the show was made by Nadia Eghbal and Mikeal Rogers due to a shift in focus from making the case for sustainability in open source to now having it as a given
• The conversation around open source sustainability has become more mainstream and there are now multiple people working on different aspects of the problem
• The original goal of RFC was to expose stories about sustaining projects, but now that those stories are out there, there's no need to continue making the case
• The show helped create a new vocabulary for discussing software infrastructure and sustainability in open source
• Nadia Eghbal's paper "Roads and Bridges" solidified the shift in how people talk about open source sustainability.
