• Proposals for GopherCon have opened until April 25th
• Key requirements for proposals: title, abstract, description, outline
• Title: creative, but not clickbait; reflects content and target audience
• Abstract: 300 characters, elevator pitch-like; highlights topic and relevance
• Description: elaborate on topic, target audience, relevance to Go community, expected impact
• Outline: roadmap of talk, demonstrates knowledge and achievability; includes timing information
• Selection criteria: relevance, clarity, correctness, achievability, impact
• Relevance: focus on Go programming language and community; explain why now
• Clarity: detail what will be covered and how; add timing to outline for structure
• Correctness: accuracy and expertise; don't let lack of knowledge stop you from submitting
• Achievability: find "sweet spot" between too much and too little content; cover one topic per 25-minute talk, two topics per 45-minute talk
• Impact: highlight expected takeaways and valuable concepts; explain how your talk will make an impression
