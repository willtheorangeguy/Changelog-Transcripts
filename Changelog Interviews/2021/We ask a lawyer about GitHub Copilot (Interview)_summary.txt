• Fair use is based on the idea that copyright should be bounded and that authors' rights should be balanced with the public interest
• The four-factor test for fair use is often applied, but the fourth factor is subjective and can be influenced by the court's discretion
• The concept of transformative use has emerged as a useful way to determine fair use in cases involving new and innovative uses of copyrighted material
• Machine learning and software-related fair use cases are complex and often involve conflicting interpretations of the law
