• Jason Bosco co-founded Typesense, an open-source search engine, in 2015 after becoming frustrated with the complexity of using ElasticSearch
• Typesense's initial goal was to create a simple search engine with a focus on speed and ease of use
• The project started as an R&D effort and was open-sourced in 2018
• Typesense differentiates itself from ElasticSearch and other search engines by storing all search indices in memory, offering fast search results at a lower cost
• The company gained traction after positioning itself as an open-source alternative to Algolia, a close-source SaaS product
• Typesense has since developed a business model and offers a paid version with hosted services
• The project has been successful, and the founders quit their full-time jobs to work on it full-time in 2020
