• GitHub's Open Source Survey was conducted to gather high-quality data on the open source community, its attitudes, experiences, and backgrounds
• The survey was a collaboration between GitHub, researchers, and industry experts to gather insights from over 5,500 respondents
• The data was collected from GitHub users and a non-random sample of communities off GitHub, and has been made publicly available
• The survey aimed to overcome the challenges of traditional surveys in gathering data from the open source community, such as self-selection bias and difficulty in accessing the right people
• The data and insights from the survey are expected to be used by researchers, industry experts, and the open source community to better understand and improve the community's dynamics and practices
