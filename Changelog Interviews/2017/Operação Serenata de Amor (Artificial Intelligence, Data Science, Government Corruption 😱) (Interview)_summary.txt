• Serenata de Amor is a project that uses data science and machine learning to uncover potential corruption in Brazilian government spending
• The project was founded by Irio and a team of friends, who were motivated by the idea of using technology to analyze public data and hold politicians accountable
• The project was crowdfunded for two months, raising almost $1,300 from 1,300 people
• The team used a process called "translating law into code" to analyze guidelines on public spending and identify potential loopholes
• The team has identified several instances of suspicious spending, including Congress people paying for meals with public funds for others
• The project aims to use artificial intelligence to monitor public spending and provide transparency and accountability in Brazilian government.
• Brazil's congressional reimbursement process allows 513 representatives to claim expenses for meals, security, and consulting services
• 219 representatives max out their monthly spending, and some claim excessive expenses, such as 30 gas tanks filled each month
• The Chamber of Deputies has four people reviewing and approving over 1,500 reimbursement claims per day
• A robot, Rosie, uses data analysis to flag potential irregularities and help identify outliers
• The process relies on human review, but Rosie's analysis can reduce the workload from 1,500 to a dozen potential irregularities
• The system still requires human judgment to determine whether flagged claims are corruption or false positives
