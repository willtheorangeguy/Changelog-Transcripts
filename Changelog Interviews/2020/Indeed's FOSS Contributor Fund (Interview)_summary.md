• The FOSS Contributor Fund is a program at Indeed where employees can nominate open-source projects used by the company, and the top project each month receives a $10,000 donation.
• The program has evolved from a single annual donation to a monthly donation, with a quarterly bonus donation of $10,000, and has given away $120,000 to open-source projects in the past year.
• The program is designed to give employees a say in which projects receive funding, and to promote engagement and visibility into the company's open-source usage.
• The company tries to stay out of the business of deciding how the funds should be used, and instead lets the project maintainers decide how to best utilize the donations.
• The program has led to additional benefits, such as hackathons and mini-hackathons with participating projects, and has helped identify employees who are passionate about open-source and can be engaged with on that topic.
• The program has also helped the company uncover footholds in other parts of the company for people who are passionate about open-source, and to find new people to engage with from that perspective.
