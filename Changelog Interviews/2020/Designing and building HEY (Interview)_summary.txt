• The idea for HEY email service originated from the problems with external communication and email management at the company.
• Jonas Downey and team explored solutions after recognizing issues with their own external communication, which was opaque and difficult to manage.
• They began by looking at how to improve their CRM tool, Highrise, but eventually decided to build a new email service that could better address their needs.
• The team iterated on prototypes and ideas, and it took about two years to go from the initial prototype to the release of HEY.
• HEY aims to solve email-related problems for businesses by providing a more streamlined and controlled email service.
• The team also plans to add features for businesses, such as custom domains and business accounts, to address the specific needs of organizations.
