• Micro-purchase: a platform that allows the government to buy small chunks of code with a $3,500 limit, using reverse-bidding to find the best price
• The platform has been successful, with an average bid of $930 and a one-dollar bid from a vendor who wanted to help

• The platform has an API that allows for purchase tracking and data analysis, and is open to the public
• Cloud.gov: a platform as a service for the government that provides a set of pre-configured services and tools to make it easier for government teams to deploy code and services
• Cloud.gov is designed to help government teams comply with regulations and memoranda, and to make it easier for them to ship code and deploy services.
• Cloud.gov was created to centralize infrastructure experience and simplify technology management for government agencies
• Cloud.gov uses the open-source platform Cloud Foundry and is designed to be scalable and secure
• The platform has been successful in reducing the burden of infrastructure management for 18F and has attracted external customers, including the Environmental Protection Agency
• Cloud.gov is going through FedRAMP certification, which is a government-wide standard for cloud security
• The long-term goal is to make cloud.gov a widely used platform for government agencies, but 18F does not want to be the exclusive provider and is working to lower the barriers for other vendors to compete
• The platform's open-source code is designed to be reusable and modular, allowing other agencies to build on top of it and reducing the need for reimplementation.
