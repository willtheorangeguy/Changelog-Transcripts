• The State of JS survey was started by Sacha Greif to help himself and others figure out the JavaScript landscape, which was overwhelming due to the many frameworks and libraries available.
• The survey aims to provide data and insights to help developers make informed decisions about what to learn and focus on.
• The surveys have been conducted annually, with the first one in 2016 and the third one in 2018.
• Sacha Greif has learned and adapted throughout the process, including addressing criticism about the survey's methodology and transparency.
• The survey relies on the community to reach influential individuals and spread the word, but this can introduce bias and limitations.
• Sacha Greif has considered reaching out to framework authors and library authors, but notes that it can be challenging to determine who is in charge and that it may introduce bias.
• The survey's results are based on self-reported data and may not accurately represent the broader developer community.
