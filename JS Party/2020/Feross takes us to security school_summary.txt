• The Samy worm, created by a user named "Samy" on MySpace, exploited a vulnerability in the platform to infect users' profiles with code that added Samy as a friend and listed him as their hero.
• The worm spread rapidly, causing an exponential increase in friend requests for Samy, eventually reaching over 1 million friends in under 20 hours.
• The creator of the worm was surprised by its rapid growth and initially thought it would be beneficial, but soon became overwhelmed by messages from users who were upset that they had been hacked.
• MySpace ultimately took action to address the issue, but not before the worm caused significant disruptions, including bringing down the site for maintenance.
• The creator of the worm was eventually contacted by the Secret Service and faced consequences for his actions.
