• Definition of generative AI discussed and debated by panelists
• Generative AI defined as AI that can create new content based on input prompts and training data, going beyond traditional pattern-matching and classification
• Previous wave of AI focused on classification/pattern-matching, while current generative AI is about translation/creation
• Breakthroughs driving the shift include large datasets and improved understanding of concepts and logic within those datasets
• Discussion of how to explain and define generative AI in simple terms for non-technical audiences
• Large language models (LLMs) such as transformers are being used for various applications
• These models require a vast amount of data to function and are only accessible to a few companies currently
• The parameters of these models, which define their characteristics, can be thought of as a massive linear algebra matrix that maps words or tokens to likely next steps
• The latent space concept is mentioned, referring to the multi-dimensional space where concepts live in the model's thinking
• The analogy between machine learning and human brain function is discussed
• Current applications and players in this space include GitHub Copilot, OpenAI (with models like Codex, GPT-3, and DALL-E), Hugging Face, and GPTZero.
