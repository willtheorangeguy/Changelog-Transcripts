• The connotations of the terms "hard skills" and "soft skills" have shifted over time, with "hard skills" implying technical difficulty and "soft skills" implying lack of rigor or definition.
• Human skills, on the other hand, refer to skills that involve interacting with humans, such as communication, empathy, and emotional management, which are learnable and tangible.
• The goal is to break down human skills into actionable tactics that can be applied and practiced to increase effectiveness and impact.
