• A backdoor was discovered in _liblzma_ (aka _XZ_), a compression library used by OpenSSH
• The exploit was deployed through authorized upstream access and was described as "the best executed supply chain attack"
• Jia Tan, the attacker, had write access to the XZ repository and signed tarballs containing the backdoor
• Analysis suggests Jia Tan's identity is unknown and may be a fictional persona
• The incident highlights patterns of attacks on open-source projects, including burnout of original maintainers and exploitation of trust by attackers
• The skills and knowledge required to write software often need to be relearned for each new project.
• Unpaid maintainers of open-source libraries may struggle with maintaining interest due to the lack of significant changes needed.
• The xz backdoor is a security issue that cannot be fully addressed by technical means alone, but rather requires counterintelligence efforts from governments and surveillance-capable entities.
