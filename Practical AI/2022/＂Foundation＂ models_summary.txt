• The concept of "grounding" in language models refers to the idea of making models more robust and flexible by incorporating multiple modalities of data.
• Grounding involves pulling together different types of data (text, images, audio) to create a more comprehensive understanding of a concept or entity.
• This approach can help reduce vulnerabilities in models that are not used to classifying certain types of input.
• The goal of grounding is to move away from single-symbolic representations and towards multimodal models that can handle multiple forms of data.
