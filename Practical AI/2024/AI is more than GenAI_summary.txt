• Foundation models and transfer learning allow for pre-training large models with millions of parameters, which can then be fine-tuned for specific tasks with smaller datasets.
• Fine-tuning involves adapting a pre-trained model to a new task by training it on a smaller dataset, rather than training from scratch.
• Larger models require more data to train and process, which stresses the need for specialized hardware like GPUs or other processors.
• Transfer learning is widely used in practice because it allows for domain-specific models with less specific training data.
• A significant portion of model processing involves feature representation or embedding, making this a transferable aspect between different tasks.
• Large foundation or base models can be made more generalizable through feature representation and increased model size
