• Discussion of an article titled "Three People-Centered Design Principles for Deep Learning".

• The importance of avoiding bias in deep learning and considering the people-centered outcome first
• Three key aspects for achieving a people-centered outcome: transparency, explainability, and reversibility
• Transparency involves understanding what you're trying to get to, being clear with intentions, and making sure data is oriented on the desired outcome
• Explainability refers to being able to understand how models make inferences and reach their conclusions
• Reversibility involves understanding how to reverse out of a model what it knows and is tied to explainability
• The three aspects are interconnected and aim to reduce the "black box" nature of deep learning models
