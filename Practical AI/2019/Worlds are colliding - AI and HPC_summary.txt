• High performance computing (HPC) applications require massive amounts of computing resources to solve computationally intense problems.
• HPC clusters are traditionally associated with expensive, government-sponsored projects and large-scale CPU deployments.

• HPC clusters differ from traditional on-prem infrastructure due to their ability to communicate efficiently and handle large amounts of data.
• The software stacks used in HPC are often closer to the hardware and can be culturally specific.
• The intersection of AI/ML with HPC is bringing new challenges, particularly with regards to virtualization and cluster communication.
• There's no one-size-fits-all approach for constructing and interfacing with clusters across different use cases.
