• The Open for Good Alliance aims to address the lack of localized training data in Africa and Asia by providing a platform for coordinating and exchanging good practices on increasing availability and quality of openly available training data.
• The alliance brings together organizations working on different types of data, including NLP and speech data.

• The Fischer Intelligence Research Lab at Macarera University focuses on applying AI to local issues in Africa, particularly in agriculture, health, infrastructure, languages, and other areas.
• The lab has contributed to projects such as:
  • Crowdsourcing crop disease data from farmers using mobile phones
  • Automating tasks for experts using machine learning and AI
  • Diagnosing plant diseases non-invasively using spectrometry light
  • Mapping crisis locations for crops, diseases, or pest infestations using radio
  • Making credit scoring accessible to historically unbanked smallholder farmers
• In health, the lab has worked on:
  • Developing artifacts for microscopes in healthcare centers
  • Using machine learning to identify parasites and count them quickly and efficiently
  • Tracking mobility of people using mobile cell phone tower data.
