• Sliced is a competitive data science game show on Twitch where four data scientists compete to create predictive models, data visualization, and find hidden features in a given dataset within two hours.
• The contestants earn points based on the performance of their model, quality of their data visualization, and discovery of "golden features" buried in the dataset.
• Nick Wan and Meg Rizdahl co-founded Sliced, with Meg having experience from working with Kaggle's competitive leaderboard format.
• The collaboration was inspired by competitive data science leaderboards and the goal is to create a community-driven platform for data scientists to learn and improve their skills.
• One of the contestants on Sliced, Ethan Douglas, has reportedly matured significantly as a data scientist through participating in the show.
