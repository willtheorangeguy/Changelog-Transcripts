• Adam Stachowiak has been producing the "Founders Talk" podcast since 2010, but paused it in 2013 due to conflicting commitments.
• He wanted to focus on building The Changelog, a company around his successful "The Changelog" podcast.
• Adam returned to producing new episodes after focusing on The Changelog and its other projects for several years.
• The show is about interviewing founders of companies to share their stories, successes, passions, and lessons learned.
• Adam mentions the community aspect of his work, including a free Slack group with over 5,000 members.
