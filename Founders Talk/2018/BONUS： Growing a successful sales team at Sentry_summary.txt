• Adam Stachowiak discusses potential podcast series on building sales teams in startups
• David Kramer shares his experience growing Century's sales team and its challenges
• Key steps for starting a sales team include hiring hardworking, trustworthy individuals who can adapt to ups and downs
• Importance of being open to adjusting preconceived notions about how sales functions operate
• Value of having experienced sales leaders who understand the industry and target audience
• Discussion on inbound vs outbound sales strategies, with Century focusing on inbound due to sufficient opportunities and preference for avoiding cold emails
• The company has a large market share with around 30,000 customers.
• The main challenge in selling is navigating the organization without making the customer mad, especially when trying to introduce the product to new departments.
• The sales team uses a standard compensation model with 50% salary and 50% on-target earnings based on sales performance.
• Exceeding quota can result in higher earnings, but there may be minimums guaranteed.
• Determining the right time to establish an actual sales team is a challenge.
• Types of sales teams: revenue generation vs. complex marketing
• Challenges of having customers interact with non-sales personnel (e.g. engineers, CEO)
• Benefits of having a dedicated sales team for higher-end sales
• Analyzing customer needs and willingness to pay more
• Hiring strategies based on analytical data and projections
• Adapting hiring plans as needed based on results
